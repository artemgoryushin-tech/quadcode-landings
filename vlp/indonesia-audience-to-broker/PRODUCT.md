## Platform

web

## Users

Primary users are Indonesian affiliates, introducing brokers, owners of trading communities and academies, performance-marketing teams, financial content creators, and entrepreneurs who already know how to acquire traders or have an audience, local market knowledge, or a partner network.

They are evaluating how to move beyond referral economics and turn an existing audience relationship into a brokerage business under their own brand. They may have little or no experience building brokerage technology, assembling providers, or structuring a launch.

## Product Purpose

Quadcode Brokerage Solutions helps qualified operators assess, plan, launch, and operate a branded brokerage business without building the underlying technology and operating infrastructure from scratch.

The landing page’s immediate job is to convert relevant visitors into a booked demo. Success is a qualified B2B lead with enough information for Quadcode to prepare a preliminary launch outline and determine the most suitable next conversation.

## Positioning

The user brings the audience, local knowledge, brand, and growth vision. Quadcode provides and coordinates the connected technology, launch infrastructure, operational framework, and regulatory-readiness support behind the brokerage brand.

The offer is introduced progressively: existing audience value first, limitations of pure referral ownership second, brokerage ownership as the next level third, and Quadcode’s ready-made operating framework as the mechanism that makes the transition viable.

## Operating Context

- Visitors commonly acquire and support audiences through affiliate campaigns, IB networks, communities, education, mobile channels, and WhatsApp.
- The short demo form collects contact details, current business model, and optional context.
- The delivery journey is framed simply as: Book a demo → review traffic and business model → present a preliminary launch outline → qualify the appropriate next step.
- Technical delivery and regulatory approvals are separate timelines.
- Bahasa Indonesia is the default language; English is available at a dedicated route.

## Capabilities and Constraints

Confirmed public capabilities and support areas:

- branded web and mobile trading experience;
- CRM, client portal, and back office;
- onboarding and KYC/AML workflows;
- payment-provider and liquidity integrations subject to the selected setup;
- dealing, risk, analytics, and reporting infrastructure;
- affiliate and IB management;
- localization, technical operations, launch-model structuring, and regulatory-readiness support.

Product and integration availability depends on the agreed product scope, target market, providers, and operating model. Specific Indonesian payments, KYC providers, commission hierarchies, or local regulatory structures must not be claimed before verification.

Prohibited public promises include: a guaranteed revenue amount or profitability, no licence required, licence included, fully compliant, or immediate acceptance of Indonesian traders. The user-approved FAQ states that owners receive `100% of the earnings from their traders`, pay Quadcode for software, and do not need additional operating purchases beyond their own website; preserve that supplied commercial wording without turning it into a guaranteed-results claim. Quadcode’s legal-entity and licensing arrangement is not disclosed publicly.

The page must preserve `/id/` and `/en/`, UTM parameters, Roistat attribution, phone validation, the CRM source `quadcode_indonesia_audience_to_broker`, server-side lead forwarding, and analytics for narrative stages, language switch, CTA, form start and submission, and WhatsApp actions.

Native Bahasa Indonesia review and legal/compliance review are required before production launch.

## Product Principles

1. Start with the value the user already owns: audience, trust, distribution, and local knowledge.
2. Expand ambition progressively from referral income to ownership of brand, customer journey, and long-term business value.
3. Make the operating mechanism concrete: connected technology and launch infrastructure, not a vague “we handle everything” promise.
4. Treat regulation, provider availability, and launch timing with precision rather than certainty theatre.
5. Give serious B2B operators one obvious next action: book a focused brokerage demo.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

The site must work across desktop and mobile web, respect reduced-motion preferences, support keyboard navigation and visible focus states, use semantic form labelling and status messaging, and maintain readable contrast. Bahasa Indonesia is the primary experience rather than a secondary translation.
